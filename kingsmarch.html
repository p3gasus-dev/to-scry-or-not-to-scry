#ResourceValue eachTattoo units (Karui ports)Runegraft units (Kalguuran ports)

Rewards scale linearly with raw shipment value, excluding dust and port quotas: about one tattoo per 100k (Karui ports, capped at 40 per shipment at roughly 4m) and about one runegraft per 400k (Kalguuran ports, capped at 100 per shipment at roughly 40m). “Tattoo units (Karui ports)” and “Runegraft units (Kalguuran ports)” divide those values by the value of each unit. Past the cap, extra rewards are rerolled 3 to 1 within the port's pool.

Port quota rewards

#RewardPrice7d

Given for completing a port quota. Kalguuran ports (Riben Fell, Pondium, Kalguur) give Ynda's Stand at character level 52+ and Cadigan's Authority at 62+. Karui ports (Ngakanu, Te Onui, Moti Aro) give Kaom's Command at 52+ and Tawhoa's Felling at 61+.

“Avg price” assumes every reward in a port's pool is equally likely, which is an assumption, not a measured drop rate. “Thin data” marks prices backed by one or two recent observations, or fewer than five listings, which can be stale.
